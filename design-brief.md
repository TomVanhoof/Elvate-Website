# elvate.be: Design brief (input for design exploration)

## What this is
Redesign of the website of **elvate**, a solo freelance web designer/developer in Belgium. Primary language is Dutch (NL), secondary English (EN). The site must be designed so both languages fit (Dutch text runs ~15-20% longer than English).

Your job: explore 2-3 distinct layout directions for the **home page** (desktop + mobile), then the supporting pages. Do NOT invent claims, testimonials, client logos or statistics. Anything in [brackets] is a placeholder.

## Business goal
Get small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) in Belgium to request a **free website audit**. That is the one primary conversion. Secondary: "See my work" and direct contact.

## Audience
Owners/managers of Belgian SMBs (shops, hospitality, craftspeople, service providers, interior/lifestyle businesses). Not technical. Time-poor, skeptical of agencies, afraid of paying too much or being locked in. They decide quickly: they scan, they don't read.

## Positioning
A personal, direct, no-nonsense designer who builds fast, clear websites that bring in customers. Solo ("ik", not "wij"). Calm, confident, professional; not corporate, not hype.

## Visual direction
Open. Keep the existing logo (lowercase "elvate" wordmark). Avoid the generic "web agency" look: no stock-photo handshakes, no gradient blobs, no icon-in-circle grids. Prefer strong typography, generous white space, and real imagery. Photography: I have a few photos (landscape/nature, published on Unsplash) for use in a visual strip. No video.

## Global elements
- **Header:** logo, nav (Home, Werk, Over mij, Contact), language switch NL/EN, CTA button "Gratis audit".
- **Sticky audit prompt:** slim floating pill or bar ("Gratis website-audit →"). Must NOT cover content or block mobile. Dismissible.
- **Footer:** email info@elvate.be, phone +32 499 70 29 28, BE 1013.537.063, links to Privacy and Voorwaarden, nav, language switch.

## Pages
1. Home
2. Werk (concept projects as short case studies)
3. Over mij
4. Gratis audit (landing page)
5. Contact
6. Privacy / Voorwaarden (plain text)

## HOME: section order and draft copy (NL)

### 1. Hero
- H1 options (design with the longest):

  - "Websites voor Belgische bedrijven die willen groeien."

### 2. Herkenbaar? (problem)
Three short pain points, one line each:
- Je website ziet er verouderd uit.
- Je wordt niet gevonden op Google.
- Bezoekers komen, maar nemen geen contact op.
Closing line: "Dat kan beter. En het hoeft niet ingewikkeld te zijn."

### 3. Wat ik doe (services)
Headline services (larger): **Websites**, **Webshops**
Supporting: **Branding** (logo + huisstijl), **Foto's** (add-on)
One line of outcome each, no paragraphs:
- Websites: "Een heldere site die snel laadt en bezoekers tot contact aanzet."
- Webshops: "Verkoop online, met betalingen en verzending die gewoon werken."
- Branding: "Een logo en huisstijl waar je jaren mee vooruit kan."
- Foto's: "Beelden die passen bij je nieuwe site." (small photo strip here)

### 4. Waarom elvate (why trust me)
Three proof points plus a photo of me [placeholder]:
- Persoonlijk: je werkt rechtstreeks met mij, geen tussenpersonen.
- Snel: sites gebouwd voor topsnelheid [Lighthouse scores: fill in after build].
- Duidelijk: vaste prijs, vaste planning, geen verrassingen.

### 5. Zo werkt het (process)
Four steps, horizontal on desktop, vertical on mobile:
1. Kennismaking: gratis gesprek van 30 minuten.
2. Concept & ontwerp: je ziet eerst hoe het eruit komt te zien.
3. Bouw & feedback: jij geeft input, ik pas aan.
4. Live & opvolging: lancering en ondersteuning.
Timeframe note: "Meestal live binnen [2-4 weken]."

### 6. Mijn werk (concept projects)
Two cards, each clearly labeled **Conceptproject**:
- **Appliqué**: interieurontwerp. Website-concept, logo en huisstijl met natuurlijke kleuren die kwaliteit uitstralen.
- **BlackBean**: koffiebrander. Website-concept in rustige, gedempte kleuren die de gezellige sfeer weerspiegelen.
Optional third card: "Jouw project hier?" with CTA. No fake client claims.

- Form: website-URL + e-mailadres + button "Start mijn audit"

## WERK page
Each concept project as a mini case study: the brief I invented, the audience, design decisions, style guide, final design screens. Label as concept work. Leave a slot ready for future real client case studies.

## OVER MIJ page
Short, personal, first person. Photo, what drives me [placeholder: personal story], how I work, then CTA to the audit. No generic values list (innovation, quality, growth).

## GRATIS AUDIT page
Single-purpose landing page: what you get, how it works (3 steps), a sample of what a report looks like [placeholder], FAQ, form. No main navigation clutter.

## Design requirements
- Mobile-first; mobile layouts for every section.
- Accessibility: contrast AA, visible focus states, readable sizes.
- Components to define for handoff: buttons (primary/secondary), cards, accordion, form fields, sticky audit pill, nav and language switch.
- Output should be easy to translate into an Astro site (static, component-based, minimal JS).
